The SKIA Process

How It Works & Privacy

Step 01

Drop Your Photo(s)

Pick any garment or object, upload 1 to 4 original resolution photos from your phone, and tell us how you want them styled.

Step 02

Approve on WhatsApp

Our studio prepares your custom digital mockup and sends it to your WhatsApp. Nothing is printed until you approve.

Step 03

Crafted & Shipped

Crafted on 240–400 GSM heavy combed cotton and infused ceramics/glass, and shipped straight to your door.

Your personal photos are protected behind your private 4-Digit PIN. We never share customer photos publicly without permission, and all raw files are permanently deleted from our production queue after printing.
